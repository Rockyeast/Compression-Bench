S2.1       Experiment Configuration on Figure 1a
In figure 1a, we utilize a self-consistent method to translate
the computation costs among quantization, pruning, SVD,
and our TSVD, as shown in Table S2. The self-consistency
can be verified by observing that the translation of TSVD
is precisely equivalent to W2Ad quantization with sparsity
consideration. For error, we select relative error of operator
norm in figure 1a.

the FLOPS of W(·) is only equal to its folding version when
considering the sparsity of W. Therefore, in figure 2b, we
have modified translation method of TSVD in table S2 as:
