ployment, demand for GPUs and new datacenter capacity
has risen sharply, while systematic tracking and reporting
of energy is often overlooked, in spite of the substantial
environmental and societal impacts of large training runs,
as well as direct financial costs of operating and developing
these systems. Many training and distillation decisions are
