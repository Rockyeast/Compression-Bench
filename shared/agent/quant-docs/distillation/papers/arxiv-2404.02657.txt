B        Evaluation details
The details for the evaluation dataset are as follows:
                                                                      4
    • Dolly: human-written instruction-response pairs . We divide the data set into 14k samples for
      training, 500 samples for validation, and 500 samples for testing following Gu et al. (2023).

    • S-NI: the test set of SUP-NATINST (Wang et al., 2022), which contains 9K samples from 119
      English tasks. In this paper, we employ the samples with ground truth responses longer than 11.

    • UnNI: dataset from Honovich et al. (2023). Similarly, we employ the samples with ground-truth
      responses longer than 11.

    We use five random seeds {10, 20, 30, 40, 50} for generation.

E        Sensitivity of µ in AKL
µ is a threshold to separate the head/tail parts. Since the sum of all logits is 1, thus we intuitively set
µ as 0.5 for head/tail split. Table 6 shows the results of more values for µ. We can find that AKL
with µ=0.45/0.55 would also outperform FKL+RKL, and even better than AKL (µ=0.5), showing the
robustness of the proposed AKL.
