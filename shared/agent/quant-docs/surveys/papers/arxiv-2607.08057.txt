Workloads. A future sKis benchmark should
cover the following three workload types to stress
temporal, spatial, and structural KV behaviors:
1. Multi-tenant or bursty online serving workloads
   to test the stability of temporal scheduling under
   high concurrency.
2. Long-context task workloads to test KV cache
   placement and migration when memory and I/O
   become bottlenecks.
3. Heterogeneous workloads, such as agent work-
   loads (Zeng et al., 2025c; Zhou et al., 2024a),
   structured editing (Zeng et al., 2025d), or
   domain-shifted workloads (Tian et al., 2024,
   2026; Li et al., 2023, 2025b; Yan et al., 2025a;
   Zeng et al., 2025a), to test the robustness of
   structural KV cache optimizations against dis-
   tribution shift.
Reporting standards. In addition to the basic in-
formation like model, hardware, and configuration,
we recommend the following reporting standards
for sKis benchmarks:
1. Performance under graduated context lengths to
   validate scalability.
2. Accuracy vs. memory curves for structural
   methods to reveal the trade-offs.
3. Detailed hardware and topology setups, espe-
   cially for temporal and spatial methods.
