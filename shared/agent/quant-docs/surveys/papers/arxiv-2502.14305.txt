D     Ethical Concerns
This paper presents a methodological study of
model compression techniques such as model
pruning and knowledge distillation. These tech-
niques aim to ensure the compressed models pro-
duce similar predictions compared to the original
ones and hence, these methods do not pose any
inherent ethical concerns. The analysis of larger
(teacher) models is out of the scope of this paper.
