A. Limitations
In practice, we identify two limitations. First, the Dense-and-Sparse decomposition requires the sparse matrix to store its
weights and index. While the weights require at least 16 bits per element, the row and column indices require at least 16
bits for each, which is non-negligible. Due to this reason, we only adopt minimum sparsity (0.5%). Besides, sparse matrix
multiplication is slow, and may harm decoding speed during inference.
