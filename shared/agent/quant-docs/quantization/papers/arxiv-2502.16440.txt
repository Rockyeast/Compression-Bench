                                         Elias Frantar1,2 , Utku Evci*,1 , Wonpyo Park1 , Neil Houlsby1 and Dan Alistarh2
                                         * Corresponding author, 1 Google DeepMind, 2 Institute of Science and Technology Austria




                                         We investigate how different compression techniques—such as weight and activation quantization,
                                         and weight sparsity—affect the scaling behavior of large language models (LLMs) during pretraining.
                                         Building on previous work showing that weight sparsity acts as a constant multiplier on model size in
                                         scaling laws, we demonstrate that this “effective parameter” scaling pattern extends to quantization
                                         as well. Specifically, we establish that weight-only quantization achieves strong parameter efficiency
                                         multipliers, while full quantization of both weights and activations shows diminishing returns at lower
arXiv:2502.16440v1 [cs.LG] 23 Feb 2025




                                         bitwidths. Our results suggest that different compression techniques can be unified under a common
                                         scaling law framework, enabling principled comparison and combination of these methods.

                                         Keywords: Model Compression, Large Language Models, Scaling Laws, Sparsity, Quantization
