6. Conclusion
We propose MoEQuant, a framework designed to address
the unique challenges of quantizing MoE LLMs. By in-
corporating Expert-Balanced Self-Sampling and Affinity-
Guided Quantization, MoEQuant extends traditional quanti-
zation methods to effectively handle both the uneven distri-
bution of calibration samples among experts and the token-
expert affinity variations introduced by gating units. Experi-
mental results show that MoEQuant achieves near-floating-
point accuracy even with low-bit quantization and signifi-
cantly improves generalizability, particularly in instruction-
finetuned models. These results underscore its potential to
substantially reduce model size and computational require-
ments, making MoE LLMs more feasible for deployment in
resource-constrained environments.
