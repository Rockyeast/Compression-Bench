E     Statement on the Use of LLMs
We report on the use of LLMs in the preparation of
this paper. The use of LLMs was strictly limited
to the role of a general-purpose writing assistant.
Specifically, we used these tools to proofread, cor-
rect grammatical errors, and rephrase sentences to
improve clarity and readability. The LLMs did not
contribute to the core scientific aspects of this work,
such as research ideation, experimental design, or
the generation of substantive content.
