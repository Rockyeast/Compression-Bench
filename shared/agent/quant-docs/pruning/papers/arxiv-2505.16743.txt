G        Sensitivity Analysis of k
The hyperparameter k controls the number of
iterations in the TRIM algorithm. A larger k
allows for a more wide-ranging exploration of
dimension-wise sparsity. Our findings indicate
that k is a robust hyperparameter, with its impact
depending on the difficulty of the pruning task. In
challenging scenarios (e.g., Qwen2.5-14B at 70%),
the iterative process is key, with performance im-
proving significantly as k approaches our default of
10 before stabilizing. Conversely, in more straight-
forward scenarios (e.g., OPT-7B at 60%), the
model’s performance is completely insensitive to k.
