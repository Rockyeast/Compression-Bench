Algorithm 1 Channel Shuffle
Input: Sparsity Metric: ξ; Sparsity pattern: M
and N .
Output: Permutations: θ.

 1: Initialize the permutations with a global shuf-
    fle: θ = Global_Naive_Shuffle(ξ, M)
 2: Reorder the ξ according to θ.
 3: Divide ξ into N blocks in the channel dimen-
    sion {ξ1 , ξ2 , · · · , ξN }.
 4: for ξn in ξ do
 5:    while True do
 6:       res = search_permutations_with_largest_
          improvement( ξn , M, N )
 7:       if res is None then
 8:           break
 9:       end if
10:       i, j = res[0], res[1]
11:       Swap channel i and channel j in ξn .
12:       Update θ.
13:    end while
14: end for
